Explain: Script → key words → cue cards

Remember ideas, not words.

🪜 Three steps

1. Script (practice only)

Write it out to plan. Then put it away. Don't memorise it.

“Last Ramadan, our team of eight served iftar to three hundred people in just two hours.”

2. Key words

Keep only the words that hold the idea: 3 to 8 per part.

Ramadan · team of 8 · 300 people · 2 hours

3. Cue card

One small card per part. Numbered. Big letters. Glance, then look up and talk.

③ STORY · Ramadan → team of 8 → 300 people → 2 hours
💡 Know your first line and your last line really well. For the middle, trust your key words and tell it in your own words.
